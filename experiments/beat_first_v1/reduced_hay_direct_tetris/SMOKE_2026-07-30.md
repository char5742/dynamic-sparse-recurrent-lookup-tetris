The same candidate panel was reused, so the loss reduction is not caused by
changing rows. Continuous compartment parameters received the global Tetris
teacher cotangent and were updated.

The fixed panel produced no event-graph gradient. Therefore this run does not
show that recurrent spike delivery is useful, only that the continuous
Reduced Hay dynamics and routing/head can learn directly. A random-row
two-update smoke did produce a nonzero graph gradient on the second update,
but that is not a fixed-panel quality result.

The reference BPTT rate is far below the existing arena trainer and is not a
CPU-performance result. The analytic-VJP/barrierless implementation remains
the next critical path.

The budget-matched Point and GRU arms each completed a one-update real
teacher wiring preflight on the same row/candidate set. Their first-call
timings include compilation and are not performance measurements. The frozen
arm was constructible, but no qualified frozen artifact was available to run;
its artifact requirement remains fail-closed.
