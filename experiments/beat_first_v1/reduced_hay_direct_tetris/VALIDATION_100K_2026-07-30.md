# Reduced Hay direct-Tetris 100k continuation — 2026-07-30

## Outcome

The retained 10k Point-SNN, Reduced Hay and diagonal-GRU checkpoints were
continued to 100,000 updates for all three independent seeds.

At equal update count:

- Reduced Hay and Point-SNN are statistically tied;
- both recurrent SNN arms outperform the smaller diagonal GRU in composite
  loss, NDCG and pairwise ranking;
- GRU retains a small top-1 lead and much higher reference throughput;
- all three held composite-loss curves are close to saturation by 50k;
- Reduced Hay uses its apical pathway strongly, uses plateau weakly and
  seed-dependently, and obtains almost no causal benefit from recurrent input.

This is not a Reduced-Hay victory over Point-SNN and it is not an
equal-wall-clock result.

## Protocol

- arms: Point-SNN, direct-Tetris Reduced Hay, diagonal GRU;
- independent model, training-order and held-panel seeds: 3;
- exact continuation: update 10,001 through 100,000;
- held milestones: 10k, 20k, 50k and 100k;
- held panel: 64 predefined `validation` rows per seed;
- candidate width: at most 40;
- state batch: 1;
- learning rate / weight decay: `2e-4` / `1e-5`;
- Julia threads / BLAS threads: 1 / 1;
- objective: canonical 22-output
  ListNet/Q/death/quantile/geometry loss;
- trainer: warmed Zygote direct-BPTT reference.

Within each seed all arms use the same training order and held panel. The
three seed processes ran concurrently. Training timing excludes held
evaluation and checkpoint writes.

The frozen 11-state arm remains unavailable because no qualified artifact
exists. No gameplay-validation or sealed seed was opened.

## Reduced Hay mechanism use

Mean activity over three seeds:

| Signal | 10k | 20k | 50k | 100k |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| workspace-active spike rate | 0.080488 | 0.098265 | 0.111787 | 0.122265 |
| soma spike rate | 0.200289 | 0.252869 | 0.313945 | 0.478756 |
| plateau fraction above 0.05 | 0.076048 | 0.090162 | 0.118080 | 0.211064 |
| plateau state mean | 0.011566 | 0.014251 | 0.021239 | 0.039027 |
| NMDA state mean | 0.085202 | 0.095647 | 0.116097 | 0.152538 |
| branch effective rank | 7.895029 | 7.525709 | 6.849788 | 6.104147 |

The compartment system is active and remains far from rank-one collapse.
However, branch rank is falling and soma firing has risen to almost one event
per two cell-cycles. The final CPU-sparse executor cannot assume the very low
event rate seen early in training.

Exact-zero 100k ablations use `delta = ablated - full`. Positive loss and
negative ranking deltas mean that the removed mechanism helped:

| Ablation | Delta loss | Delta top-1 | Delta NDCG | Delta pairwise |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| plateau state off | `+0.020348 +/- 0.029474` | `+0.005208 +/- 0.036084` | `-0.004680 +/- 0.005958` | `-0.000811 +/- 0.010682` |
| apical state off | `+0.135536 +/- 0.040475` | `-0.072917 +/- 0.032526` | `-0.029837 +/- 0.012607` | `-0.085763 +/- 0.048279` |
| recurrent input off | `+0.001859 +/- 0.005203` | `0.000000 +/- 0.031250` | `-0.000998 +/- 0.001009` | `-0.002306 +/- 0.001483` |

Interpretation:

- apical modulation has a strong causal contribution in every seed;
- plateau is recruited and helps on average, but its effect is small and
  changes sign in one seed;
- recurrent input is effectively neutral at 100k.

Thus the high-dimensional state is genuinely used, but the complete
Hay-derived recurrent mechanism has not demonstrated an advantage over the
Point control.

The continuation ratio is `2.09x` GRU/Reduced. Because the three seeds ran

An equal-wall-clock comparison must allow GRU substantially more than 100k
updates. That comparison was not performed, so the CPU objective remains
unresolved.

## Data exposure

At candidate width 40, 40,630 training rows are eligible. The deterministic
schedule shuffles without replacement, cycles, and therefore exposes every
eligible row before reuse. The 100k run covers about `2.46` passes over that
domain.

The full frozen teacher corpus contains 100,243 training rows and has observed
candidate width up to 76. Consequently, the present plateau is not caused by
unseen width-40 rows, but it does not test the full width-80 corpus. Widening
the comparison is a distinct data/architecture experiment and should precede
another same-configuration update extension.

## Decision

1. The requested 100k scale-up is complete and retained.
2. The small diagonal GRU saturates first in ranking quality.
3. Point-SNN and Reduced Hay also approach a held-loss ceiling and are tied at
   100k.
4. Reduced Hay's apical state is useful; plateau is modest; recurrent input is
   not yet useful.
5. No high-dimensional-neuron superiority or equal-wall-clock CPU victory is
   established.
6. More updates on the identical width-40 setup are low priority. The next
   scientific gate should expose the full width-80 teacher corpus and measure
   equal wall-clock, while preserving Point/Reduced comparability.
